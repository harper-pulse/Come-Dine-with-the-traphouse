Rules of the course

How pub golf works

Each bar is a hole. Each hole has a drink and a par: the number of sips it should take to finish it. Count your sips, add any penalties, and write it on the card. Lowest total after hole 8 wins. It's golf, so there's no rush: the pars are generous on purpose and nobody needs to scull anything.

Scoring

  • Your score for a hole is the number of sips to finish the drink, plus penalties.
  • Finish it in one go and it's a hole in one, but you score 1, not less. Pacing beats heroics.
  • Can't finish it? You take par + 3 for that hole and move on.
  • Each player gets one mulligan: swap that hole's drink for a lemonade or soda with no penalty.
  • Ties are settled by the Halloween Putt-off: best costume pose, judged by the bar staff at the Good Home.

Penalties and bonuses

  • Spilling your drink+1
  • Toilet break before hole 4+2
  • Taking off part of your costume+2
  • Phone out at the bar during a hole+1
  • Saying the birthday legend's real name before hole 2 (surprise!)+1
  • Skipping a water hazard+2
  • Best costume, voted at hole 1−3
  • The birthday legend, every hole−1

Water hazards

Holes 3, 5 and 7 have a water hazard. Before you tee off there, everyone drinks a full glass of water. Skip it and take +2. It's the cheapest way to make it to the Good Home in one piece.

Bar staff in NZ can't serve anyone who's intoxicated, so if one of us gets cut off, the whole team loses a bar. Eat at hole 5.

Halloween dress code

  • Full costume, all day. Costume pieces off cost strokes.
  • Masks off at the door: bouncers need to see your face and ID. Bring real ID, Halloween means busy doors.
  • No fake weapons. Most bars will turn them away.
  • Group photo on the wharf at hole 1, and on the Maungawhau platform before hole 8.

Keeping it cheap

What it should cost

One drink per hole at happy hour prices where they exist. Prices are from venue listings and will move a dollar or two either way.

Ways to spend less

  • Split the jugs at hole 1 between three or four people. It's the cheapest beer on the course.
  • Hole 2 is the only cocktail. Use your mulligan there if you're on a tight budget, a lemonade is a few dollars.
  • Have a feed at Coco's (hole 5). Happy hour food there has been $15 to $20, much cheaper than a late night kebab.
  • One person shouts each hole and everyone squares up on the day with a bank transfer. Rounds go faster and nobody holds a bar tab.
  • Load your HOP card the night before, or tap a contactless card on the gates.
